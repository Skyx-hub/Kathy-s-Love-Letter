You're my favourite girl, my little zombie, my princess, my happiness, and someone I love more than I could ever properly explain. I hope this little website makes you smile because it was made with so much love.

So here's a reminder, in case I haven't said it enough already: I LOVE YOUUUUU KATHYYYY. SO MUCH. SO SO SO MUCH. MORE THAN ALL THE STARS, MORE THAN ALL THE FLOWERS, MORE THAN ALL THE LITTLE HEARTS I COULD EVER PUT ON THIS PAGE.

Forever sending you love,
